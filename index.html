What I do

Native iOS development

Production apps in Swift and SwiftUI with MVVM, built for performance and easy upkeep. REST APIs, third-party SDKs and push notifications included.

UIKit to SwiftUI migration

Moving live apps to SwiftUI screen by screen, without freezing feature work or breaking what users rely on.

Interface design

Interfaces and prototypes in Figma and Sketch that follow Apple’s Human Interface Guidelines, then built in SwiftUI or UIKit.

Leading iOS teams

Setting technical standards, keeping architecture consistent and code quality high, mentoring junior engineers and making delivery faster and more predictable.
